## What to review

1. **Names are ROME objectives, verbatim.** They're accurate but long ("Gérer la relation client, recueillir et analyser les besoins client"). Short plain names for 84 objectives is a writing task, and it's yours.
2. **Transversal capacities make cross-sector directions.** "Gérer la relation client" groups a remote client advisor, a head of customer service, a telephone secretary, a head waiter and a funeral master of ceremonies. That's what a capacity-based direction means; whether it reads as a direction or a hodge-podge is the product call. Flagged groups are listed at the end.
3. **Fragmentation persists for broad seeds.** P5 and P9 have 15 capacities with a single role each. Those roles stay roles, outside the directions.
4. **Continuity dominates seeded profiles.** With a seed, most shared-skill rarity comes from the ~200 seed skills, so directions read "continuité" unless the answers' skills drive them (P5, P6, P7, P8, P10 each have at least one "révélée").
5. **Answer-only profiles are all "révélée"**, by construction: no seed, so nothing else to continue.

# Directions layer — prototype on data

Generated by `scripts/directions-prototype.ts` on live ROME graph + live offers (Paris). No UI, no weights changed.

## Method

Variant: capacity level **objectif**, weight **distinctive** (idf − 3, the engine's generic floor), savoirs do not drive directions, membership moyen/fort roles whose main capacity holds ≥ 0 % of their distinctive weight.

1. **Capacity** of a shared skill = its ROME *enjeu* (the 32 named domains of the official competence tree). Savoirs, outside that tree, use their ROME savoir category.
2. **Role profile** = rarity (idf) of its shared skills summed per capacity. **Driving capacities** = the top ones covering ≥ 60 % of it, at most 2.
3. **Directions** = the top 40 roles in page order, grouped by primary driving capacity; the 3 to 5 strongest groups (sum of display rank), groups of 2+ first. Name = the capacity, plus the second capacity when half the members share it.
4. **Révélée / continuité**: rarity-weighted share of the direction's shared skills (in its driving capacities) that came from quiz answers vs the seed; ≥ 50 % answers → *Révélée par vos réponses*. A skill from both counts half each.
5. **Why / trait lines** = the answer options the user chose, quoted, each traced to a skill a member role shares (checked below). Nothing is inferred about the person.
6. **Copyable titles** = ROME appellations (open data v462, Licence Ouverte 2.0), main title first, rarely-used titles dropped.
7. **Personas**: each answers every quiz scene with the option whose clusters overlap its theme most (both when tied, skipped when neither). Themes: *relation* (relation_client, contact, empathie, pedagogie, transmission, gestion_conflit, besoin_harmonie); *analyse* (analyse, systemes, investigation, diagnostic, rigueur, detection_incoherence, synthese, reporting, gestion_donnees); *terrain* (manuel, technique, terrain, resolution, execution, pragmatisme, fiabilite); *vente* (vente, persuasion, relation_client, contact, assurance, presentation); *organisation* (organisation, planification, leadership, priorisation, decision_incertitude, fiabilite, terrain).
